# Memory Match

[简体中文](README.zh.md) · **[English](README.md)**

---

Flip cards two at a time and remember where every emoji hides. Clear the board by matching all the pairs.

## How to play

- Click (or tap) a card to flip it face up
- Flip a second card: if the emojis match, both stay open; otherwise they flip back
- The game ends when every pair is matched
- The counters show your **move count** (one move = two flips) and the **timer**, which starts on your first flip

## Board sizes

| Button | Grid | Pairs |
|---|---|---|
| 4×4 | 4 × 4 | 8 |
| 6×5 | 6 × 5 | 15 |
| 8×5 | 8 × 5 | 20 |

## Run

Just open `index.html` in a browser. Zero dependencies.

The game has a built-in English / 简体中文 switch at the top; your choice is remembered in `localStorage`.
