# Typing Speed

[简体中文](README.zh.md) · **[English](README.md)**

---

Type English as fast and as accurately as you can before the clock runs out. The game grades every single character the instant you press it, so you always know exactly where you stand.

## How to play

- Press **Start**, then type the highlighted word. Correct characters turn **green**, mistakes turn **red**.
- Finish a word and the game jumps to the next one automatically; press **Space** to commit the current word early.
- **Backspace** fixes a mistake — and it never double-counts the error you just corrected.
- On phones, just tap the text area; a real input field brings up the soft keyboard.

## Modes

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Duration | 15 s · 30 s · 60 s |
| Difficulty | Easy (short words) · Normal (common words) · Hard (long words, punctuation, mixed case) |

## Live HUD

**Time left · WPM (words per minute) · Accuracy · Correct words · Combo.** Your personal best WPM is saved in `localStorage` and shown in the HUD and on the result screen.

## Run

Just open `index.html` in a browser. Zero dependencies, no build step.

The game has a built-in English / 简体中文 switch at the top; your choice is remembered.
