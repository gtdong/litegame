# Tic-Tac-Toe

[简体中文](README.zh.md) · **[English](README.md)**

---

The classic 3×3 game: line up three of your marks before your opponent does.

## How to play

- You are **X** and always move first against the computer
- Click any empty cell to place your mark
- Three in a row — horizontally, vertically or diagonally — wins the round
- The two counters track **X wins** and **O wins** across rounds

## Modes

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| vs Computer · Easy | The AI plays well but slips sometimes — beatable |
| vs Computer · Hard | Unbeatable minimax AI; the best you can get is a draw |
| Two players | Pass-and-play on the same device, O moves second |

## Run

Just open `index.html` in a browser. Zero dependencies.

The game has a built-in English / 简体中文 switch at the top; your choice is remembered in `localStorage`.
